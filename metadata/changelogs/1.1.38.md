# 1.1.38

## Changes

- New Ender Sever Array, made from a Tabula Robur and an Eye of Ender. While active, endermen, shulkers and similar creatures can't teleport within 16 blocks of it.
- Right-click a Vortex Sigil with a Tabula Rasa to make it stop pulling players. Mobs are still pulled in. Right-click it with an empty hand to take the Tabula back.
- Pack makers can choose what the Endless Quarry mines with the new `neovitae:quarry_blocks` block tag. It holds every ore, glowstone and bone blocks by default.
- The core of the Nether meteor now holds Nether Quartz Ore and Bone Blocks alongside the Ancient Debris.
- Explosive Powder and the Explosive Cells are retired. Ore processing stopped using them some time ago, so they can no longer be crafted or found in mine loot and no longer fit the Athanor. Use Cutting Fluid to turn Netherrack into Sulfur.

## Fixes

- Deus Meteo no longer leaves any filler. Blocks outside the `neovitae:quarry_blocks` tag, such as the blackstone in the Nether meteor, are swapped for one of the meteor's ores, glowstone or bone blocks.
- Addons that grant Sentient Armor experience through the Neo Vitae API can now start a new upgrade. Before, only upgrades the armor already had at level 1 or higher gained experience, so upgrades like Animus's Arcane Channeling and Source Attunement never started.
